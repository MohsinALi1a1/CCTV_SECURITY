System

How it works

  • HIGH anyone at a 🚫 No-entry area (instantly), an unknown person in a ⚠ Restricted area, or a camera went offline.
  • MEDIUM an unknown person inside while outside cameras are watching.
  • LOW a known family member entered a restricted area.
  • INFO a known person was seen, or a camera came back online.
  • 🛡 Inside + Outside: outside cameras are the first line. ⚠ Backup: an outside camera is off or missing, so inside cameras raise HIGH for any unknown person.
  • A person is called "Unknown" only after the face check had 3 s to find a match.
  • Rules: event_engine/config/rules.yml (applies on save). Events are deleted after 7 days.
